About Expertise Publications

Hello, I'm Romina.

I am a researcher and engineer specialized in hardware-aware machine learning and reconfigurable computing. As the founder of KaleidoForge and the creator of the KalEdge platform, I specialize in developing energy-efficient Edge AI solutions and FPGA architectures for real-time scientific applications.

My work focuses on making advanced technology both powerful and accessible. This involves optimizing complex deep learning models for resource-constrained platforms and designing systems that bring artificial intelligence closer to the physical world.

I am strongly committed to interdisciplinary collaboration and open science. I believe the most impactful technological advancements emerge when rigorous engineering is guided by a clear purpose and a human-centered approach.

Selected Publications

A Reconfigurable Spiking Neural Network on FPGA for Radiation Pulse Discrimination

Molina, R. S., et al.

Under review. Elsevier Nuclear Engineering and Technology, 2026.

Proposes a unified SNN architecture capable of achieving low latency for real-time radiation pulse discrimination across three different types of detectors on FPGA.

Physics-Guided Neural Network for Pile-Up Rejection on SoC–FPGAs

Molina, R. S., et al.

IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science, 2025.

Resolves severe pile-up limitations in radiation detectors using embedded physics-aware AI, maintaining spectral integrity at unprecedented count rates.

An End-to-End Workflow to Efficiently Compress and Deploy DNN Classifiers On SoC/FPGA

Molina, R. S., et al.

IEEE Embedded Systems Letters, 2023.

Presents an end-to-end compression workflow integrating pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation, significantly reducing memory footprint.
